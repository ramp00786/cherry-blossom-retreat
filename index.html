Welcome Home

A quiet little home in Kollam, where every morning smells of filter coffee, jasmine and the sea breeze.

Cherry Blossom Retreat is a family-run homestay in the heart of Kollam — the gateway to Kerala's backwaters. Here you don't just book a room; you are welcomed like family, served authentic home-style Kerala food and guided to the hidden gems of the Ashtamudi coast.

Whether you are a couple looking for calm, a family on holiday, or a traveller exploring Kerala, our clean, comfortable rooms and personal care make every stay feel like coming home.

Explore Kollam

The Gateway to Kerala's backwaters

01

Ashtamudi Lake

Kerala's second-largest lake, with eight arms of shimmering backwater, Chinese fishing nets and houseboat cruises.

02

Munroe Island

A cluster of tiny islands laced with narrow canals — best explored by country canoe at sunrise.

03

Kollam Beach & Thangassery

Golden sands, evening snacks and the red-and-white Thangassery lighthouse beside old Portuguese ruins.

04

Jatayu Earth's Center

A spectacular rock-top park at Chadayamangalam, home to one of the world's largest bird sculptures.

05

Palaruvi & Thenmala

A milky waterfall in the forest and Kerala's first planned eco-tourism destination, a scenic day trip away.
