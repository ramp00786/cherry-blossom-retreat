Welcome Home

A bright, brand-new home in Kollam, with spotless rooms, a green courtyard and the sea breeze just a short drive away.

Cherry Blossom Retreat is a homestay at Elavanthi, near Apsara Junction in Kollam — just 3.2 km from Kollam Railway Station — the gateway to Kerala's backwaters. Polished marble floors, airy air-conditioned rooms, private bathrooms and a quiet paved courtyard shaded by a mango tree make it an easy place to unwind.

Guests rate the stay 5.0 ★ on Google (41 reviews) and 9.7 / 10 on Agoda, with a perfect 10 for cleanliness, facilities and service. Whether you are a couple, a family or a traveller exploring Kerala, the hosts are always happy to help.

Explore Kollam

The Gateway to Kerala's backwaters

01

Ashtamudi Lake

~38 min drive

Kerala's second-largest lake, with eight arms of shimmering backwater, Chinese fishing nets and houseboat cruises.

02

Munroe Island

~55 min drive

A cluster of tiny islands laced with narrow canals — best explored by country canoe at sunrise.

03

Kollam Beach & Thangassery

12–18 min drive

Golden sands, evening snacks and the red-and-white Thangassery lighthouse beside old Portuguese ruins.

04

Jatayu Earth's Center

A spectacular rock-top park at Chadayamangalam, home to one of the world's largest bird sculptures.

05

Palaruvi & Thenmala

A milky waterfall in the forest and Kerala's first planned eco-tourism destination, a scenic day trip away.
